    FIELD MANUAL / 01

    Flight manual.

    Survive three sectors and destroy each flagship. Move through gaps, collect power from fallen aircraft, and keep the chain alive.

    AURELIA / OPTIONAL REACTORS — In phases two and three, aim direct fire at the glowing side reactor. Breaking it opens a marked cover lane for 3.2 seconds and grants WAKE. The final reactor also disables the right spiral cannon for the rest of the fight. You can keep attacking the main hull, or dismantle its weapons from the side.

    CROWN / TRACKING LANCE — Let the marked lane track your position, then move out when it locks. The locked lane stays fixed before firing. Rolls can cross it; any ship special vents the charge.

    KEYBOARDWASD / ARROWS — MOVESPACE / Z — FIRESHIFT — FOCUSX — SHIP SPECIALC — BARREL ROLLP / ESC — PAUSE
    CONTROLLER & TOUCHLEFT STICK / D-PAD — MOVEA / RT — FIRELB / LT — FOCUSB — SPECIALX — ROLLSTART — PAUSEHANGAR: D-PAD UP / DOWN — MENUTOUCHDRAG TO MOVE & FIRERELATIVE DRAG KEEPS THE CRAFT VISIBLESECOND FINGER — FOCUS / SPECIAL / ROLL

    ARSENAL — P: uncapped gun power · E: escort pair · M: sidewinders · S: shield charge · R: six seconds of reflection · ★: ship signature · B: special. Kestrel gains Hellfire Wings; Manta gains Hammer Torpedoes; Wraith gains Ghost Orbitals. A hit scatters your equipment and power as salvage. Sweep it back up before nine seconds expire; blinking tokens are about to vanish. The × number is the value you recover.

    Hold FOCUS to slow movement, concentrate fire and draw in nearby pickups. Only the bright aircraft core takes a hit.

    GRAZE BULLETS TO CHARGE WAKE. ROLL WHEN CHARGED TO CONVERT BULLETS INTO SCORE.
